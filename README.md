 Overview

This project analyses job change across 16 UK city-regions and both broad (1-digit SIC) and granular (2-digit SIC) industry sectors, answering questions like:

- Which industries grew or shrank the most between 2011 and 2014?
- Which cities saw the strongest employment growth?
- How does that growth break down by sub-industry within each city?

 Dashboard components

- Sum of Jobs in 2014 by City** — bubble chart sized by total employment, highlighting London's dominant share (4.68m jobs) against the other 15 cities.
- Change by Sub-Industries** — an icicle/treemap view drilling from broad sector into sub-industry, surfacing detail like Real Estate Activities (+65,387 jobs).
- Change by Industry** — a ranked bar chart of net job change per industry, showing Administrative & Support Services and Professional, Scientific & Technical Activities as the strongest gainers, and Manufacturing as the clearest decliner.
- Average % Change by City (map) — a geographic view of average percentage employment change per city, with an interactive city filter.

Data source

EMSI (Economic Modelling Specialists International) UK employment data, comparing job counts by city and industry (SIC 1-digit and 2-digit classifications) between 2011 and 2014.

 Tools used

Tableau · Excel (data cleaning & validation) · SQL-style aggregation logic
